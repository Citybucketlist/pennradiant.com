brand.

Key facts to anchor on:
- **Brand:** Penn Radiant Products, pennradiant.com — "Industrial Infrared Heating & Cooling Solutions, Built to Last in the USA, Since 1965." Founded by Del Lieb.
- **Primary lines:** Space-Ray (spaceray.com) and Heat Wagon (heatwagon.com) heaters + parts.
- **Colors:** Brand Red `#ED1D24` + PRP Blue `#0A6FB0` are the two core colors. Near-black bands for application photography. Use orange only as an earned infrared-glow accent.
- **Type:** Myriad Pro → substituted with Source Sans 3. Heavy, often italic headlines; UPPERCASE two-weight section labels; red ▸ spec bullets.
- **Surfaces are flat** — hairline borders, dividers and gray fills, not drop shadows (the red logo banner is the one exception).
- **Voice:** plain-spoken, industrial, confident, American. No emoji. Specs sell.
- **Signature motif:** the right-leaning angled banner/tag (logo, section headers, blue/red category tags).

If the user invokes this skill without other guidance, ask what they want to build or
design, ask a few clarifying questions, and act as an expert designer who outputs HTML
artifacts _or_ production code, depending on the need.
